# 01 · Product Vision

**For** college students preparing for campus placements **who** lose track of deadlines, eligibility rules and their own preparation progress, **the College Placement Portal** is a web application **that** centralizes placement profiles, preparation tracking, company discovery, eligibility checking and application status tracking.

**Unlike** spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups and notice boards, **our product** gives each student a live readiness score, tells them instantly which companies they can apply to, and gives placement officers a single place to manage the whole process.

## Vision statement
> "Every student should know exactly where they stand — what to improve, which companies they can target, and where each application is — at any moment."

## Success criteria
- Students check readiness and eligibility without asking the placement office
- Officers manage companies and statuses from one dashboard
- No missed deadlines due to missing information
- The system is demonstrable end-to-end in a 10-minute viva

## Scope boundaries (out of scope)
Payments, real email delivery, job scraping, mobile apps, AI features, microservices/Kubernetes.
